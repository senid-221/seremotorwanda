1Fungura konti ya motari

Kuri motari: nta payment — umuyobozi wa SERE Moto ni we ukwemeza.

  1. Ku rupapuro, kanda Rider hejuru.
  2. Uzuza Amazina, Indangamuntu (imibare 16), TIN, Plaque (RAB 123 A) na Telefone (07xxxxxxxx).
  3. Hitamo Ubwoko bw'ikinyabiziga: Moto cyangwa Imodoka/Taksi.
  4. Kanda Ohereza code kuri telefone, wandike code y'imibare 6 wahawe, ukande Emeza code.
  5. Kanda Ohereza application — ibi bikora iyo telefone yamaze kwemezwa.
  6. Application iba itegereje kwemezwa. Hanyuma kuri Urubuga rw'umumotari hitamo Aho ukorera (intara → akarere → umurenge → akagari). Umuyobozi ashyiraho serivise (moto zishyurwa ku giciro cya RURA; imodoka zishyiraho RWF/km), akayemeza cyangwa akayitera igiti avuga impamvu.

Kwinjira nyuma: Injira → telefone + code ya SMS. Nta jamo ry'ibanga rikenewe. Iyo umaze kwemezwa, Urubuga rw'umumotari rufunguka.

2Shaka motari cyangwa taxi

Kuri wowe ukeneye: nta konti ikenewe kugira ngo ushake.

  1. Kanda Passenger hejuru — app ihita ikusaba Location.
  2. Ikakubaza kimwe kimwe: INTARA YAWE? → AKARERE KAWE? → UMURENGE WAWE? → AKAGARI KAWE? → UMUDUGUDU WAWE? Akagari n'umudugudu ushobora kubisimbuka ukanda Komeza.
  3. Hanyuma iza iti WOOW! TWAMAZE KUMENYA AHO URI. — kanda Komeza — ikubaza iti NONEHO USHAKA KUJYA HEHE?: wandika aho ujya, uhitemo Ikinyabiziga (Moto / Imodoka) na Serivise, ukande Nshaka Motari.
  4. Buri kadi yerekana amanota, intera iri hagati yanyu, ikimenyetso cya LIVE n'igiciro. Izina ry'umurenge werekanwe ni aho telefone yabo iheruka kugaragara — ni hafi yawo, si muri wo neza.
  5. Umupingu utwaye umugenzi werekanwa nk'Afite umugenzi; Ntwara kurwo rupapuro ntikora.

Nta n'umwe ugaragara hafi? Abamotari bakoresha GPS gusa iyo app yabo ifunguye — gerageza akarere kandi.

3Ntwara

Demand ibikwa, WhatsApp igahita ifunguka ifite ubutumwa bwanditse.

  1. Kanda Ntwara ku motari wahisemo — idirishya ryo kwuzuza rirafunguka.
  2. Uzuza Izina ryawe na Telefone kugira ngo motari ikubaze.
  3. Hitamo Icyo ushaka: Gutwarwa (abagenzi) cyangwa Kohereza Imizigo (Derivari).
  4. Andika Uva na Ujya — intera ibarwa ku mihanda nyayo (OpenStreetMap); niba izina ry'ahantu ritabonetse, shyiramo Intera (km) ubwawe.
  5. Kanda Ntwara. Ushobora no guhita uhamagara ukoresheje Hamagara.
  6. Umumotari yemera cyangwa akanira kuri Urubuga rw'umumotari — akanda Emera cyangwa Inga kuri tabi ya Ziriho.

Igiciro cy'umupingu kigenwa na RURA: 400 RWF kuri km 2 za mbere, 117 RWF/km kugeza kuri 40 km, 187 RWF/km iyo byarenze; iminota 10 ya mbere yo gutegereza ni ubuntu. Urugero: 5,5 km = 810 RWF.

4Kwishyura nyuma y'urugendo

Ubwishyura butangizwa n'uwatwawe iyo urugendo rwemejwe cyangwa narwo rurarangiye.

  1. Muri Ibyange, kuri tabi ya Inzira, kanda Kwishyura ku rugendo.
  2. Hitamo MTN MoMo (telefone yishyura) cyangwa Karita (Paystack — imeyili).
  3. Kanda Fungura urupapa rw'ishyura. MoMo igusaba kwemeza kuri telefone yawe.
  4. Ikarita y'urugendo ihita yerekana Byishyuwe cyangwa Birategereje.
  5. Umumotari ubwe abona amafaranga ye kuri tabi ya Amafaranga ho kuri Urubuga rw'umumotari.

Platform ikuramo 5% by'igiciro; motari ikagumana 95%. Umumotari awkanda Rangiza urugendo ahita aba offline — yongera kuboneka iyo akoze Nditeguye.
